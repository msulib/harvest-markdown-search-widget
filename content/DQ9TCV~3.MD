# Date and Nut Bar

![date and nut bars in a stack](/foodprodevlab/images/banners/date-nut-bars_banner.jpg)

Courtesy of MSU Communications

## Ingredients

Product1 |  Amount |  Unit  
---|---|---  
Medjool dates (fresh, whole, pitted, organic) |  1 1/2 |  cup  
Cashews (roasted) |  1 |  cup  
Cranberries (dried, sweetened) |  1 |  cup  
Morton's Kosher Salt |  1/2 |  tsp  
Kirkland Pure Vanilla Extract |  2 |  tsp  
[MPP Richlea Lentil Protein Concentrate](https://montanapure.us) |  1/2 |  cup  
  
1The MSU Food Product Development Lab does not endorse any ingredient suppliers, but for culinary and scientific specificity, we tested the recipe using the brand name products listed above.

## Instructions

  1. Roast lentil protein at 400°F for 10 minutes until just golden and fragrant.
  2. Combine and blend all ingredients in a food processor.
  3. Press the mixture into a pan lined with parchment; refrigerate for a minimum of 2 hours.
  4. Cut into squares and enjoy.
