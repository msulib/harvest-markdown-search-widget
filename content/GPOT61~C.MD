# Gluten-Free Pumpkin Muffins or Bread

![three muffins](/foodprodevlab/images/banners/pumpkin-muffins_banner.jpg)

Courtesy of MSU Communications

## Ingredients

Product1 |  Amount |  Unit  
---|---|---  
[MPP Red Crimson Lentil Protein](https://montanapure.us/) |  2 |  cup  
Bob's Red Mill Gluten Free 1:1 Baking Flour |  1 |  cup  
Libby's Pumpkin Puree |  1 1/2 |  cup  
Eggs |  2 |  whole  
Milk (whole) |  1/4 |  cup  
Wesson Canola Oil |  1/2 |  cup  
C&H Granulated White Sugar |  1/2 |  cup  
C&H Golden Brown Sugar |  1/2 |  cup  
Kirkland Pure Vanilla Extract |  1 |  tsp  
Kirkland Ground Cinnamon |  1 |  Tbsp  
McCormick Pumpkin Pie Spice |  2 |  tsp  
Kosher Salt |  1/2 |  tsp  
Baking soda |  1 |  tsp  
  
1The MSU Food Product Development Lab does not endorse any ingredient suppliers, but for culinary and scientific specificity, we  tested this recipe using the brand names mentioned above.

## Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Combine wet ingredients (pumpkin puree, eggs, milk, oil, sugars, vanilla extract); mix well.
  3. Combine dry ingredients (flour, lentil protein, cinnamon, pumpkin spice, salt, baking soda); mix well.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients; mix until just combined.
  5. Add batter to muffin pan or loaf pan. Bake muffins in oven at 425°F for 5 minutes; reduce heat to 375°F for 17-19 minutes. If in a loaf pan, bake until a knife inserted in the center comes out clean.
