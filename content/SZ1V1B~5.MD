# Shortbread Cracker

## Ingredients

Product1 |  Amount |  Unit  
---|---|---  
[MPP Richlea Lentil Protein Concentrate](https://montanapure.us) |  1 1/2 |  cup  
Wheat Montana All-Purpose Flour |  1 |  cup  
C&H Granulated White Sugar |  1/4 |  cup  
Egg yolks |  2 |     
Cream (heavy) |  1/4 cup + 2 Tbsp |     
Salt (kosher) |  1 |  tsp  
Butter (sweet cream, unsalted) |  1 1/2 |  cup  
  
1The MSU Food Product Development Lab does not endorse any ingredient suppliers, but for culinary and scientific specificity, we  tested this recipe using the brand name products listed above.

## Instructions

  1. Sift together dry ingredients (flour, lentil protein, salt)
  2. Cut in butter into dry ingredients until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Fold in cream and egg yolks.
  4. Roll dough into desired size logs; wrap with plastic wrap.
  5. Chill dough logs for 2 hours.
  6. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  7. Slice log into pieces of desired thickness (1/8-1/4 inch).
  8. Arrange pieces on cookie sheet and bake for 15 minutes.
