# Gluten-Free Frying Batter

## Ingredients

Product1 |  Amount |  Unit  
---|---|---  
Bob's Red Mill Brown Rice Flour |  1/2 |  cup  
Bob's Red Mill Sweet White Rice Flour |  1/4 |  cup  
[MPP Richlea Lentil Starch](https://montanapure.us) |  3/4 |  cup  
Sugar (white, granulated) |  1 |  tsp  
Salt (kosher) |  1/2 |  tsp  
Pepper (black, ground) |  1/4 |  tsp  
Beer or seltzer (cold) |  1 |  cup  
Oil for frying (e.g., peanut, corn, sunflower) |    |     
  
1The MSU Food Product Development Lab does not endorse any ingredient suppliers, but for culinary and scientific specificity, we  tested this recipe using the brand name products listed above.

## Instructions

  1. Combine dry ingredients (flours, lentil starch, sugar, pepper, salt) in a bowl.
  2. Preheat oil in frying pan.
  3. Immediately before frying, add cold club soda to the bowl of dry ingredients; whisk slowly.
  4. Lightly coat items to be fried with flour or starch, then dip in wet batter.
